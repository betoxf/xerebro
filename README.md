# Xerebro

**AI-powered Mac control from anywhere.**

Xerebro lets you control your Mac remotely through natural language. Chat with AI from the web or iOS app, and it executes commands on your Mac - taking screenshots, clicking, typing, running terminal commands, and more.

---

## How It Works

```
┌─────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐
│  You        │────▶│  Xerebro    │────▶│  Your Mac   │
│  (Web/iOS)  │◀────│  Cloud      │◀────│  (Daemon)   │
└─────────────┘     └─────────────┘     └─────────────┘
```

1. **Sign in** at [xerebro.app](https://xerebro.app) or the iOS app
2. **Install the Mac app** and pair it to your account
3. **Chat with AI** - describe what you want done
4. **AI executes** actions on your Mac and reports back

## Features

### Remote Mac Control
- **Screenshots** - See your Mac screen from anywhere
- **Mouse control** - Click anywhere with grid-based precision
- **Keyboard input** - Type text and use shortcuts
- **Terminal** - Run shell commands and scripts
- **Apps** - Open and control any application

### AI-Powered
- **Natural language** - Just describe what you want
- **Screen understanding** - AI sees and comprehends your display
- **Multi-step tasks** - Breaks down complex requests into actions
- **Error recovery** - Handles unexpected situations

### Cross-Platform
- **Web app** - Control from any browser
- **iOS app** - Native iPhone/iPad experience
- **macOS daemon** - Lightweight background service

## Requirements

- **macOS 13.0+** (Ventura or later)
- **Apple Silicon or Intel Mac**
- Internet connection

---

## Quick Start

1. **Create account** at [xerebro.app](https://xerebro.app)
2. **Download** the macOS app from [Releases](https://github.com/betoxf/xerebro/releases/latest)
3. **Install & pair** - drag to Applications, open, and sign in
4. **Grant permissions** - allow Accessibility and Screen Recording
5. **Start chatting** - use web or iOS app to control your Mac

---

## Privacy & Security

- **End-to-end control** - Your Mac, your commands
- **Secure authentication** - Apple Sign-In
- **Local execution** - Commands run on your Mac only
- **No screen storage** - Screenshots are ephemeral
